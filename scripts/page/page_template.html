Ranged Doomstack Research

A stat-only evaluation of every recruitable ranged unit in the game for armies that field a single unit type at a time, ranged, ground or flying. No community opinion is used anywhere on this page. Every number is read from the Patch 9.0 unit, projectile, explosion and entity tables, and every verdict is computed from the formulas in the Model section, which you can re-weight live.

What is in the data

For each unit the page holds the full stat line: recruitment cost, upkeep, build time, in-game tier, models or vehicles per unit (Ultra size), hit points per entity and per unit, armour, shield block chance, leadership, run and fly speed, mass, entity radius, melee attack and defence, charge bonus, melee weapon damage split, accuracy and reload skill, resistances (missile, physical, magic, fire, ward), ammunition in shots, and the primary projectile: base and armour-piercing damage, bonus versus infantry and large, effective range, base reload time, projectiles per shot, shots per volley, burst size and delay, calibration distance and area (the game's accuracy model), spread, marksmanship, maximum elevation (whether it fires over friendly lines), collision radius, magical and ignition flags, penetration depth and size cap, and the explosion record: detonation radius, detonation damage, armour-piercing detonation damage and force. Bombs and activated shots that live on abilities (Gyrobomber bombs, Sky-junk bombs, Spear of Grungni) are listed on the unit's sheet as well.

Unit counts use Ultra unit size. Weapon teams fire one weapon per two models. Artillery fires per engine, cavalry per mount, flying machines per vehicle. Regiments of Renown, Grudge Settlers, Elector Count troops, Blessed spawnings, Amethyst units, Tamurkhan's chieftain units and summons are kept in the ledger but marked special and excluded from rankings by default, because you cannot field nineteen of them.

The model

The ranking is a weighted sum of six normalised scores. Each score is derived from the stats with the formulas below, against a target profile you can change. Weights default to a spam army's priorities and can be moved with the sliders in the ledger controls.

1. Damage per hit versus armour

armour roll u ~ Uniform(0.5, 1.0) reduction = min(1, armour × u / 100) expected multiplier on base damage m(A) = 1 − E[reduction] A ≤ 100: m = 1 − 0.75·A/100 A > 100: computed numerically (reduction saturates at 100%) per-hit damage = base × m(A) + AP (+ bonus vs large or infantry, split in the weapon's base/AP ratio) shields: small-arms shots from the front are blocked with probability block%; artillery and explosions ignore shields; Shieldbreaker contact sets block to 0

This is the game's armour rule as stated on the wiki's Damage page: armour reduces base damage by a random fraction between half and the full armour value, capped at 100, and never touches armour-piercing damage.

2. Hits per projectile

direct and arcing shots vs a formation: hits = 1 + min(penetration, 3) when the penetration size cap allows the target size explosion vs a formation: extra models hit = min(models in target, 16, density × π × radius²) density = 0.35 models/m²; blast damage on a formation is scaled by 0.6 for edge models overlap: blast hits from one volley are capped at the formation size; the whole volley is capped at the formation's HP pool (models × HP), so the surplus is overkill explosion vs a single entity: 1 extra hit at explosion damage single-entity targets: hit chance = min(1, (target radius / scatter radius)²) scatter radius = (calibration area / 2) × (engagement range / calibration distance) × (1 − accuracy skill/100) engagement range = min(effective range, 150 m), or 70% of range for artillery

Calibration distance and area are the actual accuracy records: at the calibration distance, shots land inside a circle of the given diameter. Scatter grows linearly beyond it. Big single targets are easy; a lord on foot inside a blob is not, so blob targets assume every projectile hits something.

3. Rate and sustain

effective reload = base reload × (1 − reload skill/100) projectiles per volley = projectiles per shot × shots per volley × burst size unit damage per second (DPS) = firing entities × projectiles per volley × hits × per-hit damage × hit chance ÷ effective reload volleys = ammunition ÷ shots per volley time on trigger = volleys × effective reload battle damage = DPS × time on trigger volleys before contact = (range ÷ closing speed) ÷ effective reload

4. Durability, standoff, campaign

effective HP vs missiles = HP ÷ (1 − missile res) ÷ (1 − ward) × (1 + block/200) effective HP vs melee = HP ÷ (0.35 + 0.65 × m(own armour)) ÷ (1 − physical res) ÷ (1 − ward) (assumes attackers with 35% AP share) standoff = range × (fires over friends ? 1.0 : 0.85) × (fire whilst moving ? 1.15 : 1) × (flying ? 1.2 : 1) flyer answer = can the weapon shoot a flying target: lobbed artillery 0, flat artillery 0.3, small arms 1, then × hit chance vs a large flyer cost = DPS per 100 upkeep, with a penalty for build time above 1 turn and for unit caps

5. Composite

Each score is rescaled to 0–100 by rank within the shown set, then combined with the weights. The default weights are: damage versus the chosen target 30, battle damage 15, durability 15, standoff 15, flyer answer 10, cost efficiency 15. Change the target profile or the weights and the ledger re-ranks instantly.

Assumptions the data cannot settle

  • Reload skill is applied as a flat percentage reduction of reload time. The tables store it as an integer; the game's exact curve is not published.
  • Bonus versus large or infantry is split between base and AP in the weapon's own ratio. The game adds it as flat damage; the split affects only armoured targets.
  • Formation density of 0.35 models per square metre, a 16-model cap per blast and a 0.6 edge factor are estimates for explosion coverage on a formation two to four ranks deep; blast hits per volley are capped at the formation size and a volley cannot deal more than the formation's HP pool. Single targets take full blast damage once.
  • Ability bombs (Gyrobombers, Sky-junk, gyrocopter grenades) are listed on the sheet but not added to DPS, because their use count and recharge make them a per-battle burst, which is shown separately as "ability burst".
  • Permanent campaign buffs are a separate layer, off by default. "Campaign ceiling" applies every technology in the race's tree, the generic lord whose army skills help the unit most (all ranks taken), and fixed-plateau mechanics (Forbidden Workshop, Scrap, Forge and adventure upgrades). "Ceiling + legendary lord" lets a legendary lord compete with the generic lords on skills, lord trait and faction-only techs, and picks whoever scores highest for the unit. Faction-specific mechanics (Forbidden Workshop, Malakai's adventure rewards, Imperial Supply) count for a generic lord, because a generic lord can serve in that faction, but for a legendary lord only his own faction's mechanics count. Rank 7 veteran perks are excluded unless ticked, because the unit has to earn them. Buildings, landmarks, items, hero skills, rites and temporary bundles are not applied. Each unit's sheet lists exactly what was applied and the other lord options.
  • Buff arithmetic follows the game's effect types: "missile strength" multiplies base and AP damage separately, "reload time reduction" adds to reload skill, ammunition, range and speed are percentages, armour, leadership, resistances and bonus-vs are flat adds. Unit experience itself is not applied.
  • Reload times below 5 seconds (throwing weapons and continuous-fire weapons stored as 0) are clamped to 5 seconds, the shortest practical firing cycle once animation time is counted.
