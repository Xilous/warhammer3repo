# Thanquol's Masterplans: unlocks, upgrades and what you gain, start to endgame

For **Clan Scruten (Thanquol)**, Immortal Empires, game 9.0.4, alongside `GUIDE_Stormfiend_Ratling_Cannons.md`.
Every number is read from the game's scripts and DB tables, not from the wiki (sources at the end). This guide lists what
Masterplans can give you and when; how you run each plan is up to you. The assistant never acts in the Masterplans
panel.

## 1. What Masterplans can give you

| Gain | Comes from | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Free armies** | Kill-Stab plans | Spawns under the target settlement with no recruitment or upkeep cost, and can only attack that settlement. Declares war on the owner when it completes (unless rebels) |
| **Gold, Warpstone, food** | Steal-Take plans | Richer targets pay more. Shop upgrades add much bigger cards (section 4) |
| **Verminlords with an army** | Great-Smart: Summon Verminlord (Grey Seer only) | Corruptor, Deceiver, Warbringer or **Warpseer**. Arrives at rank 1, or up to rank 10 with shop upgrades |
| **A ready-made Under-City** | Great-Smart: Under-City (any Underling) | Up to 4 Under-Empire buildings already built: assassins, kidnappers, Warpstone crafting, Warpstone refinery |
| **A province-wide strike** | Great-Smart: Entreat the Horned Rat (Grey Seer) | Devastates a province. Costs 100 Warpstone |
| **Story rewards** (single player) | Story missions, most of them Masterplans | 3000 gold, 850 Warpstone, and the Morskittar Engine (section 3) |
| **Levelled Underlings** | Every successful plan | 250 XP per card drawn. Underlings level up and get skill points like a character |

Every plan costs 50 Warpstone to start. Kill-Stab and Steal-Take draw 2 cards per turn; Great-Smart draws 1. A plan
can complete from its 2nd turn.

## 2. Unlocks, start to endgame

| Order | Unlock | Single player | Multiplayer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Masterplans, the **Grey Seer** Underling, **Kill-Stab** plans, the shop | First settlement captured | Same |
| 2 | **Steal-Take** plans | Story mission 1 (Kill-Stab on Zhufbar) | After your first successful plan |
| 3 | **Second Underling** (pick Master Assassin, Warlord or Warlock Master) | Dilemma "First, a Rat's Paw", after story mission 2 | Same dilemma, turn 6 |
| 4 | **Summon Verminlord** (Grey Seer) | Buy Verminlord Study I in the shop | Same |
| 5 | **Under-City** plans | Story mission 5 | Turn 31 |
| 6 | **All four Underlings** | Story mission 5 | Turn 36 |
| 7 | **Morskittar Engine** at Zhufbar | Story mission 5 (mission 4 hands over the plans) | **Never**: locked for the whole game |
| 8 | **Entreat the Horned Rat** | After Thanquol's final battle | Turn 81 |

The four Underlings and the shop each one uses:

| Underling | Shop | Built-in trick (innate skill) | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grey Seer | Scryanak's Temple | Better Rats! (swap a Kill-Stab Reward for a better unit) | Summoning Verminlords; flat gold and Warpstone cards later |
| Warlock Master | Tailclaw's Labs | Prototype (copy a Reward) | **Warpstone (Drillers)**, gold, Ratling Cannon Stormfiends in Kill-Stab |
| Warlord | Reekjaw's Warrens | none | Strong Kill-Stab armies: Stormvermin, melee Stormfiends, a Warlock Engineer |
| Master Assassin | Chitterstab's Hideouts | Versatile Approach I (remove a Complication) | The biggest gold cards (Clan Eshin Thieves), safer plans |
| all of them | Thanquol's Planning Rooms | | Better basic loot, rank-ups for Kill-Stab armies, final-battle support |

For the Stormfiend build, the **Warlock Master** is the best second Underling: his Drillers are the cheapest Warpstone
upgrade in the game (600 including the purchase they need first), and he is the only one who puts Ratling Cannon
Stormfiends in Kill-Stab armies. The other two arrive later anyway.

## 3. Start to endgame: what to gain at each stage

### Start: first capture
- **Unlocks:** Masterplans, the Grey Seer, Kill-Stab.
- **Gain: a free army for Zhufbar.** Zhufbar is the last region of Black Water. Owning the whole province allows the
  Expansionist Planning commandment, which the Dominating Scheme rite needs, so your Warpseer supply starts. It is also
  the Morskittar Engine's site. In single player it is story mission 1: **1000 gold, 50 Warpstone, Steal-Take unlocked**.
  (In testing, the plan army attacking alone lost to Zhufbar's garrison; it works best alongside Thanquol's army.)
- **Buy (Grey Seer):** Superior Rats! 200 and Warlord Clan's Tunnel I 300. Cheap, and they are the two purchases
  Verminlord Study I needs.

### Early: the second Underling
- **Unlocks:** Steal-Take (if not already), the second Underling.
- **Gain: a Warpstone engine.** Pick the Warlock Master and buy Warpfire Equipment Racks 300 + **Clan Skryre
  Drillers 300**. Every Drillers card drawn in a Steal-Take plan pays **+150 Warpstone** (Veteran +250, Elite +450,
  300 each).
- **Single player:** story mission 3 (2 Kill-Stab + 2 Steal-Take) pays **1000 gold and 50 Warpstone** twice; story
  mission 4 (Steal-Take on Skavenblight) pays **500 Warpstone** and the Morskittar notes.

### Mid
- **Gain: Warpseer lords with armies.** Buy **Verminlord Study I** (800). The Grey Seer can now run Summon Verminlord;
  pick the Warpseer. This is on top of the rite's one Verminlord every 15 turns.
- **Gain: Stormfiend armies for free.** Buy **Heavy Weapons Facility I** (600): the Warlock's Kill-Stab armies get
  Stormfiends (Ratling Cannons) instead of Rat Ogres. Good for secondary sieges your doomstack shouldn't spend
  ammunition on.
- **Unlocks:** Under-City (single player mission 5 / multiplayer turn 31) and all four Underlings (mission 5 / turn 36).
  An Under-City in a neighbour can come with a Warpstone Refinery (40 Warpstone per turn) or Crafting (20 / 40) already
  built.
- **Single player:** mission 5 (Thanquol at rank 13 and the "Death From Within" battle) unlocks the **Morskittar
  Engine**. At level 5 it gives **+10% missile strength** (base and armour-piercing), +50 research per turn and +500
  income, for 28,500 gold over 15 turns with Zhufbar at tier 5. Mission 6 adds 200 Warpstone.

### Late
- **Gain: rank-10 Warpseers.** Verminlord Study II to V (600 each): summoned Verminlords arrive at rank 3, 5, 7, then 10.
- **Gain: big gold and Warpstone cards** (section 4 has all of them):
  - Warlock: Clan Skryre Engineers, gold worth 100% / 150% / 200% of the target's income.
  - Master Assassin: Clan Eshin Thieves, gold worth 100% / 200% / 300% of the target's income plus 20% / 40% / 60% in
    Warpstone.
  - Grey Seer: "Magical" Items, 1000 / 1500 / 2000 gold; Clan Rictus Miners, 100 Warpstone plus 20% / 40% / 60% of
    the target's income in Warpstone.
  - Planning Rooms: Training: Warp-Finding and Thievery, which raise the basic loot cards every Underling draws.
- **Gain: stronger free armies.** Training lines in the Planning Rooms give Kill-Stab units +3 / +6 / +9 ranks.
  Warlord upgrades add Stormvermin, melee Stormfiends and a Warlock Engineer to his Kill-Stab armies.

### Endgame
- **Single player:** Final Battle Support (six of them, 1000 Warpstone each) for Thanquol's final battle; after it,
  Entreat the Horned Rat.
- **Multiplayer:** Entreat the Horned Rat from turn 81.
- **Faster summons:** Hasten Ritual (Grey Seer, 1000) is a trick that draws a Plan Component, so summon plans finish
  sooner.

## 6. Multiplayer in short

- No story missions: no mission rewards (850 Warpstone, 3000 gold) and **no Morskittar Engine** (the game locks it at
  the start of every Immortal Empires game, and only story mission 5 removes the lock).
- Unlocks come by turn: second Underling turn 6, Under-City turn 31, all Underlings turn 36, Entreat turn 81.
  Steal-Take after your first successful plan. Summon Verminlord through the shop, as in single player.
- Warpstone comes only from the Lair, Under-Empire buildings and Steal-Take, so the Drillers matter even more.
- A Kill-Stab declares war on the target's owner when it completes: keep it away from friends' settlements.
